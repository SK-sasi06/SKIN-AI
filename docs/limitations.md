# Limitations, Ethics & Safety Disclaimers

## 1. Technical Limitations
- **Lighting & Resolution**: Shadows or over-exposed flash photography may distort texture and redness detection. Users are advised to use neutral diffuse daylight.
- **Single Focal Plane**: Standard 2D camera photos cannot assess subcutaneous dermal depth or tactile skin turgor.

## 2. Ethical Considerations
- **No Biometric Identification**: The model extracts aesthetic cosmetic surface characteristics only and does **not** perform facial recognition, identity verification, or user profiling.
- **Local Data Privacy**: Uploaded analysis images are processed in-memory and discarded unless the user explicitly logs in and saves a scan report.

## 3. Medical & Regulatory Disclaimer
- ALSKINCARE AI is an informational and cosmetic routine optimization system.
- It is **not** an FDA-cleared or CE-marked medical device.
- It does **not** provide diagnoses for dermatological diseases, neoplasms, or serious infectious skin disorders.
